# Portfolio Roadmap — AI Tools Cheatsheets

## Now — strengthen authority

- keep the highest-value tool/config/workflow pages current and source-backed
- preserve evidence labels on fast-changing claims
- keep contributor tasks narrow, testable and maintainer-friendly
- cross-link readers to architecture/implementation repos when they need runnable systems

## Next — architecture learning bridges

Add concise bridge pages that explain when readers should move from tool usage to system design:

- AI frontend state and trust → `frontend-ai-patterns`
- Angular copilot UX → `angular-ai-copilot-starter`
- full-stack copilot integration → `ngx-copilot-platform`
- managed-agent lifecycle/governance → `agent-studio`

## Next — maintainer health

- continue periodic link/source validation
- maintain a small good-first-issue backlog with clear acceptance criteria
- publish releases around meaningful content cohorts rather than trivial edits
- track which pages are most stale/most used so maintenance effort follows user value

## Later — team handbook toolkit

Strengthen the fork-for-your-team path with templates for approved tools, MCP inventory, permission matrix, verification policy and internal AI engineering workflows.

## Quality gates

- no unsupported claim without evidence labeling
- security-sensitive guidance prefers least privilege and human review
- content duplication across portfolio repos is intentional and minimal
